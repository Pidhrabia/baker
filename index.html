Handcrafted Philosophy

Flour, Water, Salt, Time. Nothing Artificial.

Founded in 2019, Flour & Hearth was built on the belief that real bread requires patience. We source heirloom wheat directly from sustainable mills across Tennessee and allow our wild sourdough starters to ferment naturally for 36 hours.

Every pastry, tart, and celebration cake is shaped by hand in small batches every morning. When our deck ovens open at 7:00 AM, you are tasting honest traditional craftsmanship.
